Global Privacy & Data Retention Policy

Last updated: September 2026 • Document Ref: ARD-POL-2026-V2

1. Strict Multi-Tenant Data Isolation

The Ardhnarishwar Global Attendance Platform guarantees strict organizational tenant boundaries. Data belonging to Client Company A is cryptographically and logically isolated from Client Company B across all database collections, search indexes, and reporting engines.

2. Biometric Face AI Privacy & Retention

Our facial recognition technology runs entirely on-premise without transmitting unencrypted biometrics to external 3rd-party vision APIs. Facial landmark encodings are stored exclusively as 128-dimensional numerical vectors and can be deleted immediately upon employee offboarding or account removal.

3. Geolocation & Satellite GPS Telemetry

GPS coordinates captured during Field Check-In/Check-Out are recorded solely for the purpose of validating attendance within authorized geofences and client visit verification. We do not engage in continuous background location tracking.

4. Immutable Audit Trails & Compliance

System administrative events, employee creation, policy updates, and attendance corrections are recorded in immutable audit logs ensuring SOC-2 and corporate compliance integrity.

5. Contact Data Protection Officer

For privacy inquiries, GDPR data deletion requests, or tenant isolation audits, contact the Ardhnarishwar Security Office at: security@ardhnarishwar.com.
